Statement on AI

I don't use generative AI in any of my blog posts, apps, open-source code, or other personal projects. It's not part of my creative process in any way.

Generative AI is a tool for producing textureless mediocrity at scale. It's the enemy of curiosity, craftsmanship, personal growth, connection, intentionality, accountability, and truth. I have no use for it.

I've spent my life learning to build, maintain, and reason about complex systems. Sometimes these systems are expressed in the written word, other times in code. In either case, my north star has always been quality: a system's aptness and terseness in expressing the concepts it was meant to embody. Quality is, without exception, a product of time, hard work, and deep understanding. There are no shortcuts.

Generative AI cannot produce high-quality work. Furthermore, its effect on the world has been overwhelmingly negative in almost every respect. As such, all my personal projects will continue to be made with human ingenuity, effort, and attention to detail.
